# Product brief
Authoritative specification: ../REQUIREMENTS.md, version 1.0. Confirmed pilot: Roman Catholic. Vestry gives authorised parish staff a private workspace to preserve originals, maintain people/households, enter registers and independently review official entries. Archivists can transcribe without any AI dependency. The design prioritises clerical speed, clear status and original provenance.

Primary journeys: member lookup → draft entry → submission → independent approval; volume → original upload → manual/local transcription → human review → imported draft → official approval; approved entry → authority-approved template → audited PDF issue.

Civil registration, canonical validity and legal decisions are not automated. Optional finance/pastoral/property modules are later releases. See RELEASE_STATUS.md for scope and remaining work.
